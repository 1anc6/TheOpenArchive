0.999… = 1: A Paradox That Isn't

By Lanchenba Mutum · 2026 · Added 12 October 2026NEW

A careful demonstration that 0.999… is exactly equal to 1, with an explanation of why the result so often surprises students.

This shows that the infinite sum represented by 0.999… is exactly equal to 1. It does not merely approach 1—it reaches it in a precise mathematical sense.
