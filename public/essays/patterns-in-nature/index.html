How Do Patterns in Nature Relate to Mathematical Laws?

By Lanchenba Mutum · 2026 · Added 12 October 2026NEW

An examination of Alan Turing's work on morphogenesis, showing how the spots and stripes of animal markings arise from reaction–diffusion between activators and inhibitors.

Many people associate Alan Turing with the Enigma machine, but in this essay, I shall be exploring one of his later works where he discovered the connection between nature and mathematics.
