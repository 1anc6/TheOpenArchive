            Lanchenba Mutum

            Author · The Open Archive

            Lanchenba Mutum writes for the curious reader: essays across mathematics, science and whatever else sparks a good question, and honest write-ups of the projects he builds. Each essay begins with something familiar and fun, a favourite show, a song, a party game, and follows it with care and precision, until a playful question becomes a doorway into deep ideas: the different sizes of infinity, why 0.999… = 1, the limits of proof, chance, and the patterns woven through nature. Each project write-up shows how the thing works, what it measured, what broke and what didn't ship. The aim is simple: rigorous ideas, told clearly, so that anyone with a question can follow the reasoning all the way through.

            From the Author

            Hi, I'm Lanchenba Mutum, or just Lance if you'd rather not wrestle with all those syllables (most people don't, and honestly, fair lol). Welcome in, make yourself comfortable. This is where I keep my writing. Most of the essays started the same way: I was minding my own business, enjoying a show, a game, a song, or even just a number, when a question ambushed me and refused to leave. Chasing those questions is how I ended up writing about limits, the sizes of infinity, Gödel and game theory. These days I also build things, like MetaProof, an app that weighs the evidence on whether an image is real, edited or AI-generated, and I write those up here too: how they work, the numbers, and the parts that went wrong. Next up: more science, more projects, and wherever else my curiosity drags me (it usually wins lol).

            I write everything with you in mind, the curious reader who doesn't want to be buried in jargon. No specialist background required, and there are far fewer scary symbols than it looks, I promise. I've rewritten the tricky bits more times than I'd like to admit, so that each step actually makes sense. If you get lost anyway, that's on me, not you, so tell me and I'll fix it.

            Want a PDF? Tap Request PDF and send me a quick email, I'm genuinely happy to share. Questions, corrections, wild ideas for the next essay or project, or just a hello are always welcome: lanchenba613500@gmail.com. Thanks for stopping by, it really does mean a lot.

            Using this site

            • Reading online. Select any essay title to open its page, then choose Read essay. Your place is saved automatically, so you may return to it at any time.
            • Highlights. In the reader, select text and choose Highlight. Your highlights stay on the page and are collected in the Library.
            • Reading list. Select the bookmark on an essay to add it to your reading list in the Library.
            • PDFs. Every essay is free to read here. For the PDF, select Request PDF on an essay page, in the reader or in the Library, and send the short email that opens. If you don't use an email app, choose Copy the request instead.
            • New writing. Select Email me new writing at the bottom of the Essays page and send the email that opens. You'll hear about the next essay or project write-up as soon as it's out.
            • Reading comfort. The reader offers light, sepia and dark modes. Optional background music and sound effects can be switched on or off in the header.
